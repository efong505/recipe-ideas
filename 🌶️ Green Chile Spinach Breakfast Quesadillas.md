# 🌶️ Green Chile Spinach Breakfast Quesadillas

**Servings:** 2
**Prep time:** 5 minutes
**Cook time:** 10–12 minutes

## Ingredients

* 4 large eggs
* 4 medium flour tortillas
* 1 cup shredded cheese
* ½–¾ cup frozen spinach, thawed
* 3–4 tablespoons roasted green chile salsa, plus more for serving
* 1 tablespoon butter or olive oil
* ¼ teaspoon garlic powder
* Salt and black pepper to taste

## Make the filling

**1. Get the spinach dry.**
Thaw the spinach and squeeze it **really well** to remove excess water. This is important—you don't want soggy quesadillas.

**2. Sauté the spinach.**
Heat about ½ tablespoon butter in a skillet over medium heat. Add the spinach, garlic powder, a little salt and pepper, and cook for about **2 minutes**.

**3. Add the eggs.**
Beat the eggs, pour them over the spinach, and gently scramble until they're **just barely set**. Don't completely cook them because they'll continue cooking inside the quesadilla.

**4. Bring in the green chile.**
Take the skillet off the heat and stir about **2 tablespoons of the green chile salsa** into the eggs.

## Build the quesadillas

Put a tortilla in a lightly buttered skillet over medium-low heat.

Add:

**cheese → spinach/egg mixture → spoonful of green chile → a little more cheese**

Put another tortilla on top.

Cook for about **2–3 minutes per side**, until golden and crispy and the cheese is melted.

Cut into wedges and serve with additional green chile salsa for dipping.

## 🔥 One little upgrade

If you have **cumin**, put just a tiny pinch (about **⅛ teaspoon**) into the eggs. Green chile + cumin + eggs + cheese + spinach works extremely well.

And if you happen to have **potatoes left from yesterday**, I'd change this again: crispy diced potatoes would turn this into a seriously good **New Mexico-style green chile breakfast quesadilla**.
