# Southwest Turkey, Sweet Potato & Lentil Chili

A hearty, budget-friendly batch meal using **1 lb of reduced-price ground turkey**; lentils and beans stretch the meat into multiple meals.

**Equipment:** Power Pressure Cooker XL  
**Servings:** 8  
**Prep:** 15 minutes  
**Total:** about 60–75 minutes  
**Historical estimated batch cost:** $12–14 (ingredient value, not an additional purchase)

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground turkey (85% lean)
- 2¼ lb sweet potatoes, peeled and diced
- 1 cup dry brown or green lentils, rinsed
- 2 cans (15 oz each) beans, drained and rinsed (black or pinto)
- 2 cans (14–15 oz each) diced tomatoes
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 1 bag frozen mixed vegetables (about 12 oz)
- 2½ cups water or low-sodium chicken broth
- 2 tablespoons chili powder
- 1½ teaspoons ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- ½ teaspoon dried oregano
- ¼ teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional)
- Salt and black pepper, to taste
- Shredded cheddar, for serving (optional)

## Power Pressure Cooker XL directions

1. **Brown turkey.** Select Sauté. Add ground turkey and diced onion; cook 5–7 minutes, breaking up the meat, until browned.
2. **Cook lentils.** Stir in rinsed lentils, 2½ cups water or broth, and all seasonings. Scrape the bottom to loosen any browned bits. Lock the lid.
3. **Pressure-cook.** Use the Beans/Lentils setting for approximately 12–15 minutes at pressure, adjusting if the model permits. Keep cooker below the halfway fill mark for lentils.
4. **Release safely.** Allow 10–15 minutes natural pressure release, then carefully vent remaining pressure following the cooker manual.
5. **Add remaining ingredients.** Stir in sweet potatoes, tomatoes with juices, drained beans, and frozen vegetables.
6. **Simmer uncovered.** Select Sauté and cook with lid off 20–25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until sweet potatoes are fork-tender. Add water if too thick.
7. **Finish.** Taste, adjust salt or chili spices, and serve with optional cheddar. On day two, serve over rice or a baked potato.

## Safety, storage and provenance

Ground turkey must reach **165°F**. Refrigerate leftovers in shallow containers within two hours; use within 3–4 days or freeze.
