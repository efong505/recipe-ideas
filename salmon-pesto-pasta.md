
Yes. A Ninja blender or Ninja single-serve cup can make pesto.

For the best texture:

1. Add the pine nuts or pecans, garlic, Parmesan, lemon juice, salt, and pepper first.


2. Add the basil and press it down gently.


3. Pour in about half of the olive oil.


4. Use short pulses, stopping to scrape down the sides.


5. Add the remaining oil gradually until the pesto reaches the consistency you like.



Avoid running it continuously for too long. That can heat the basil, darken it, and make the pesto overly smooth.

A small Ninja cup is often better than the large pitcher because the ingredients stay closer to the blades. When using the large pitcher, you may need to stop and scrape several times.
