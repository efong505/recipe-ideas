# Apple-Cinnamon French Toast with Bacon, Eggs & Avocado

**Servings:** 2
**Prep time:** 10 minutes
**Cook time:** 20–25 minutes
**Total time:** About 30–35 minutes

## Ingredients

### Apple-Cinnamon French Toast

* 4 slices bread
* 2 large eggs
* ½ cup milk
* 1 teaspoon vanilla extract, optional
* ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon
* Pinch of salt
* 1 tablespoon butter, for the skillet

### Warm Cinnamon Apples

* 1 medium apple, thinly sliced or diced
* 1 tablespoon butter
* ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon
* 1–2 teaspoons brown sugar, maple syrup, or honey, optional
* 1 tablespoon water, if needed

### Bacon, Eggs & Avocado

* 4–6 slices bacon
* 4 large eggs
* 1 avocado
* Salt, to taste
* Black pepper, to taste
* Butter or oil for cooking the eggs, if needed

### Fresh Fruit

* 1–2 cups cantaloupe, cubed
* 1 banana, sliced

### Optional for Serving

* Maple syrup
* Powdered sugar
* Extra cinnamon
* Butter

## Instructions

### 1. Cook the Bacon

Cook the bacon in a skillet over medium heat until crisp, turning as needed.

Transfer to a paper towel-lined plate and set aside.

If you like, reserve a small amount of the bacon fat for cooking your eggs.

### 2. Make the Warm Cinnamon Apples

While the bacon cooks, melt **1 tablespoon butter** in a small skillet over medium heat.

Add the sliced apple and cook for about **3–4 minutes**, stirring occasionally.

Sprinkle with:

* ½ teaspoon cinnamon
* Brown sugar, maple syrup, or honey, if using

Cook for another **3–5 minutes**, until the apples are tender but still hold their shape.

If the skillet becomes dry before the apples soften, add about **1 tablespoon water**.

Reduce the heat to low or remove from the heat and cover to keep warm.

### 3. Prepare the French Toast Custard

In a shallow bowl, whisk together:

* 2 eggs
* ½ cup milk
* 1 teaspoon vanilla
* ½ teaspoon cinnamon
* Pinch of salt

Whisk until well combined.

### 4. Cook the French Toast

Melt about **1 tablespoon butter** in a large skillet or griddle over medium heat.

Dip each slice of bread into the egg mixture, coating both sides. Let excess mixture drip off.

Place the bread in the skillet and cook for about **2–3 minutes per side**, until golden brown and cooked through.

Keep the finished slices warm while you cook the remaining pieces.

### 5. Cook the Eggs

Cook the remaining **4 eggs** however you prefer.

For this breakfast, **over-easy or over-medium eggs** work especially well because the yolk is great with the bacon and avocado.

Season with salt and black pepper.

### 6. Prepare the Avocado

Cut the avocado in half, remove the pit, and slice the flesh.

Divide between the two plates.

Season lightly with:

* Salt
* Black pepper

A small squeeze of lemon or lime is also good if you happen to have one.

### 7. Make the Fruit Bowl

Combine the cubed cantaloupe and sliced banana.

Divide between two small bowls or place directly on the breakfast plates.

## Assemble

For each person, serve:

* **2 slices apple-cinnamon French toast**
* Half of the warm cinnamon apples spooned over the French toast
* **2–3 slices bacon**
* **2 eggs**
* **½ avocado**
* Cantaloupe and banana

Drizzle the French toast lightly with maple syrup if desired.

## Best Way to Eat It

I’d keep the plate intentionally **half sweet and half savory**.

Take bites of the warm apple French toast on its own, then alternate with the **bacon, egg, and avocado**. The salty bacon and creamy avocado cut through the sweetness of the apples and French toast really nicely.

**One small upgrade:** crumble one slice of bacon over the avocado and eggs instead of serving all the bacon whole. It ties the savory side of the plate together.
