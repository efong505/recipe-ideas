# Garlic-Parmesan Turkey & Broccoli Penne

**Servings:** 4–6
**Prep time:** 15 minutes
**Cook time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** About 40 minutes

## Ingredients

* 1 pound ground turkey
* 12 ounces penne pasta
* 2–3 cups frozen broccoli
* 2 medium tomatoes, diced
* 3 tablespoons olive oil, divided
* 4 cloves garlic, minced
* ¾ cup grated Parmesan cheese, plus more for serving
* 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
* ½ teaspoon garlic powder
* ½ teaspoon onion powder
* ½ teaspoon salt, plus more for pasta water
* ½ teaspoon black pepper
* ¼ teaspoon red pepper flakes, optional
* ¾–1 cup reserved pasta water
* 1 tablespoon butter, optional but recommended
* 1–2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice, optional
* Lemon zest, optional

## Instructions

1. **Cook the penne.** Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil. Cook the penne until just al dente. Before draining, reserve **at least 1 cup of pasta water**.

2. **Cook the broccoli.** While the pasta cooks, heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the frozen broccoli and cook for about **6–8 minutes**, stirring occasionally, until heated through and beginning to brown. Season lightly with salt and pepper. Remove it from the skillet and set aside.

3. **Brown the turkey.** Add another tablespoon of olive oil to the same skillet. Add the ground turkey and season with Italian seasoning, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes if using. Cook for **6–8 minutes**, breaking it into small pieces. Let some of the turkey sit against the pan for a minute or two before stirring so you get a few browned, crispy edges.

4. **Add the garlic.** Reduce the heat to medium. Add the remaining tablespoon of olive oil and minced garlic. Cook for about **30–60 seconds**, just until fragrant.

5. **Build the sauce.** Add the drained penne and about **½ cup pasta water** to the skillet. Add the Parmesan a little at a time while tossing continuously. Add the butter, if using. The Parmesan, olive oil, butter, and starchy pasta water should emulsify into a light, glossy sauce.

6. **Add the broccoli.** Return the broccoli to the pan and toss everything together. Add additional pasta water a few tablespoons at a time if the pasta looks dry.

7. **Add the tomatoes last.** Turn the heat to low or completely off. Fold in the diced tomatoes and let them warm for **1–2 minutes**. You don't want to cook them down into tomato sauce—they're meant to stay fresh and juicy.

8. **Finish and taste.** Add lemon juice or zest if you have it. Taste and adjust Parmesan, salt, black pepper, or red pepper flakes.

## A couple tricks that will make it especially good

**Don't skip the pasta water.** That's what turns the Parmesan and olive oil into an actual sauce rather than leaving you with dry pasta covered in grated cheese.

Also, let the turkey get **genuinely browned** before moving it around too much. Ground turkey can be bland when it's simply cooked through, but those browned edges add a lot of flavor.

And I particularly like the **lemon finish** here. Even 1 tablespoon of lemon juice wakes up the Parmesan, garlic, broccoli, and fresh tomatoes without making the dish taste lemony.
