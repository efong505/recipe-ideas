# Homemade Pesto for Either Recipe

**Makes enough for one 8-serving pasta dish.**

## Ingredients

* 4 packed cups fresh basil leaves
* **⅔ cup pine nuts**

  * Substitute: **⅔ cup pecans**, preferably lightly toasted
* 4 garlic cloves
* 1 cup finely grated Parmesan cheese
* 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
* 1 cup extra-virgin olive oil
* ½ teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
* ½ teaspoon black pepper

## Instructions

1. Place the basil, pine nuts, garlic, Parmesan, lemon juice, salt, and pepper in a food processor.
2. Pulse until everything is finely chopped.
3. With the processor running, slowly drizzle in the olive oil.
4. Process until combined but still slightly textured.
5. Taste and adjust the salt, lemon juice, or Parmesan as needed.

**Pecan option:** Toast the pecans in a dry skillet over medium-low heat for 3–5 minutes. Let them cool completely before making the pesto.

# Recipe 1: Whole-Wheat Pesto Pasta with Salmon

**Servings:** 8
**Preparation time:** 20 minutes
**Cooking time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

* 24 ounces whole-wheat spaghetti, linguine, or penne
* 2 to 2½ pounds salmon fillets
* 2 tablespoons olive oil
* 2 medium zucchini, sliced into half-moons
* 2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
* 4 garlic cloves, minced
* ½ teaspoon salt, divided
* ½ teaspoon black pepper, divided
* ¼ teaspoon crushed red-pepper flakes, optional
* 1 batch homemade pesto
* 1 cup reserved pasta water
* 1 lemon
* Additional Parmesan for serving

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the salmon:**
   Preheat the oven to **400°F**. Place the salmon on a lined baking sheet. Brush it with 1 tablespoon olive oil and season with half of the salt and pepper.

2. **Bake the salmon:**
   Bake for approximately **12–16 minutes**, depending on the thickness, until the salmon flakes easily and reaches your preferred doneness. Set it aside for several minutes, then break it into large pieces.

3. **Cook the pasta:**
   While the salmon cooks, boil the pasta in well-salted water until al dente. Reserve **1 cup of pasta water**, then drain.

4. **Cook the vegetables:**
   Heat the remaining tablespoon of olive oil in a large skillet or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the zucchini and cook for 4–5 minutes.

5. Add the tomatoes, garlic, remaining salt and pepper, and optional red-pepper flakes. Cook for another 2–3 minutes.

6. **Combine the pasta and pesto:**
   Remove the pan from direct heat. Add the drained pasta and pesto. Begin with ½ cup of pasta water and toss thoroughly. Add more pasta water as necessary to create a smooth sauce.

7. Gently fold in the salmon pieces, taking care not to break them apart too much.

8. Finish with the juice of half the lemon. Serve with additional lemon wedges and grated Parmesan.

### Helpful Salmon Tip

Keep the salmon in fairly large pieces when folding it into the pasta. Smaller flakes can disappear into the sauce and become overworked.

# Recipe 2: Whole-Wheat Pesto Pasta with Italian Sausage

**Servings:** 8
**Preparation time:** 20 minutes
**Cooking time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

* 24 ounces whole-wheat penne, rotini, rigatoni, or spaghetti
* 2 pounds Italian sausage

  * Mild, sweet, hot, chicken, or turkey sausage will work
* 1 tablespoon olive oil, if needed
* 2 medium zucchini, sliced into half-moons
* 2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
* 1 medium onion, diced
* 4 garlic cloves, minced
* ¼ teaspoon crushed red-pepper flakes, optional
* 1 batch homemade pesto
* 1 cup reserved pasta water
* 1–2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
* Additional Parmesan for serving
* Salt and black pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. **Cook the pasta:**
   Boil the pasta in well-salted water until al dente. Reserve **1 cup of pasta water**, then drain.

2. **Brown the sausage:**
   Remove the sausage from its casings, when applicable. Place it in a large skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high heat.

3. Cook for approximately **7–9 minutes**, breaking it into bite-sized pieces, until thoroughly browned and no longer pink. Transfer the sausage to a plate.

4. Carefully drain excess grease, leaving approximately 1 tablespoon in the pan. Add a little olive oil when using lean chicken or turkey sausage.

5. **Cook the vegetables:**
   Add the onion and zucchini. Cook for 5–6 minutes, stirring occasionally.

6. Add the tomatoes, garlic, and optional red-pepper flakes. Cook for another 2–3 minutes.

7. Return the sausage to the pan and reduce the heat to low.

8. **Add the pesto:**
   Remove the pan from direct heat. Add the pasta and pesto, followed by ½ cup of reserved pasta water.

9. Toss thoroughly, adding more pasta water as needed until the pesto evenly coats the pasta.

10. Stir in 1 tablespoon of lemon juice. Taste before adding more lemon, salt, or pepper because the sausage and Parmesan may already provide plenty of salt.

11. Serve with additional Parmesan.

## Best Pasta Choices

* **Salmon:** Linguine, spaghetti, or penne
* **Sausage:** Penne, rotini, or rigatoni
* **Best all-purpose choice for both:** Whole-wheat penne

The pesto should be stirred in **after removing the pan from direct heat**. High heat can darken the basil and cause the olive oil and cheese to separate.
