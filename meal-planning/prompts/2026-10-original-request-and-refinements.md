# First-run prompting history — October 2026

This file records the **request progression** that made the first ten-day plan work. It is a **public-safe, lightly normalized reconstruction**, **not a verbatim transcript**. Personal payment timing and family identifiers are omitted.

## Initial request (normalized)

> I would like recipe ideas for 10 days that are inexpensive, creative, healthy, and easy to make—especially one-pot dishes for the Power Pressure Cooker XL. The grocery budget is no more than $120 for the ten-day period. Please include costs for recipes and ingredients.

The first draft proposed **ten different dinners**, with potential leftovers.

## Most important correction

> A single meal can be spread across a few days. We are not necessarily cooking every day. Review the plan to accommodate this.

This transformed the project from a daily-dinner list into **five large-batch dishes**, about **eight planned portions per batch**, to supply two people with lunch and dinner for two days per batch.

## During-shopping constraints that improved accuracy

- The chosen store was **Smith's Food & Drug**.
- On-hand groceries included roughly **27 eggs**, **3¾ lb potatoes**, **¾ gallon milk**, peanut butter, oats, bread, and carrots.
- New purchases included **10 lb chicken leg quarters for $7.99**, **one 18-count egg carton for $2.69**, and a **1 lb $2.25 markdown turkey** package.
- A photo of the shelf tag showed **yellow onions, 3 lb for $2.49 with card**.
- Actual receipt later showed **$97.16 for 43 items**; turkey's inclusion in that total was not independently verified from the images.
- The critical correction was that there was **only 1 lb of ground turkey**, not multiple pounds or the 3 lb regular-price package pictured nearby.

## After-meal feedback

- Overall very positive assessment of the budget, flavor, batch structure and pressure-cooker use.
- The **Southwest Turkey, Sweet Potato & Lentil Chili** was explicitly praised.
- The **Peanut, Chickpea & Lentil Vegetable Stew** was rejected on taste and should **not** be repeated.
- Future household preference: **no vegan/vegetarian main dishes**; legumes may remain supporting ingredients.

## Prompt lesson

The best result came from **conversational refinement in stages**: initial concept → two-day batch schedule → actual pantry → actual store and shelf prices → receipt reconciliation → exact recipes → household taste retrospective. The reusable prompt codifies that process instead of trying to invent final recipes and final shopping costs all at once.
