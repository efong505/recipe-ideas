# New Mexico Green Chile Chicken & Potato Stew

Hearty, flavorful, and budget-friendly one-pot comfort food.

**Equipment:** Power Pressure Cooker XL  
**Servings:** 8  
**Time:** 65–80 minutes  
**Historical estimated batch cost:** $8–10

## Ingredients

- 3 lb bone-in chicken leg quarters
- 2 lb russet potatoes, cut into ¾-inch cubes
- 3 medium carrots, sliced
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- ⅓ head green cabbage, chopped
- 1 can pinto beans (15 oz), drained and rinsed
- 1 can diced tomatoes (14–15 oz), with juices
- 1 can diced green chiles (4–7 oz), to taste
- 3 cups chicken broth
- 1½ teaspoons ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- ½ teaspoon smoked paprika
- ½ teaspoon dried oregano
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Power Pressure Cooker XL directions

1. **Load cooker.** Place chicken, onion, green chiles, tomatoes, broth, cumin, garlic, paprika, and oregano in inner pot.
2. **Pressure-cook.** Secure lid. Select Meat/Chicken and cook at pressure for approximately 18–20 minutes.
3. **Release and shred.** Let pressure release naturally for 10–15 minutes, then safely release remaining pressure. Remove chicken; verify **165°F**, discard bones and skin, and shred.
4. **Simmer vegetables.** Add potatoes, carrots, and cabbage to broth. Use uncovered Sauté for 18–25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until tender.
5. **Finish stew.** Stir in pinto beans and shredded chicken. Simmer 3–5 minutes until heated through.
6. **Adjust and serve.** Mash a few potatoes against the pot to thicken. Taste and adjust salt, pepper and spices.

## Serving and storage

Serve as a hearty bowl or with whole-grain toast; optionally top with a little cheddar or cilantro. Day two: serve thicker, or over rice. Stay below cooker's maximum fill line. Add more green chile if desired. Refrigerate leftovers within two hours; use within 3–4 days.
