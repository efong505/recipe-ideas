# Instant Pot Hamburger Soup

**Adapted from:** Salt & Lavender — Instant Pot Hamburger Soup  
**Servings:** 4–6  
**Prep time:** 10 minutes  
**Cook time:** 17 minutes  
**Pressure-building time:** About 15 minutes  
**Total time:** About 42 minutes

## Ingredients

* 1/2 tablespoon olive oil
* 1/2 medium onion, chopped
* 2 celery stalks, chopped
* 1 pound extra-lean ground beef (about 95% lean)
* 3 cloves garlic, minced
* 2 heaping tablespoons tomato paste
* 1 (28-ounce) can diced tomatoes, including juices
* 4 cups beef broth
* 2 large Russet potatoes, diced
* 1/4 teaspoon Italian seasoning, or more to taste
* Salt, to taste
* Black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Set the Instant Pot to **Sauté**. Add the olive oil, chopped onion, and celery. Cook for about **4 minutes**, stirring occasionally.

2. Add the ground beef and minced garlic. Continue cooking for approximately **5 minutes**, breaking the beef apart as it cooks, until browned.

3. If you're using extra-lean beef, you can leave the small amount of rendered fat in the pot for flavor. If using fattier beef, drain off excess grease.

4. Stir the tomato paste into the beef mixture until incorporated.

5. Add the diced tomatoes with their juices, beef broth, diced potatoes, and Italian seasoning. Stir everything together.

6. Secure the Instant Pot lid and set the pressure valve to **Sealing**.

7. Cook on **High Pressure for 8 minutes**.

8. When the cooking cycle finishes, carefully perform a **quick pressure release**.

9. Open the lid once the pressure has fully released. Taste the soup and season with salt and black pepper as desired.

10. Serve hot.

## Notes

* Anywhere from **1 to 1 1/2 pounds of ground beef** can be used.
* For a lower-sodium soup, use **low-sodium beef broth**.
* Russet potatoes work especially well, but Yukon Gold or red potatoes can also be used.
* The pressure-building time is approximately 15 minutes and is included in the total preparation time.

## Optional Additions

For extra vegetables, consider adding:

* Chopped carrots
* Corn
* Green beans
* Spinach

If using spinach, stir it into the finished soup after releasing the pressure and allow it to wilt before serving.

## Storage

Store leftover soup in a covered container in the refrigerator for **3–4 days**.

The soup can also be frozen for up to **3 months**.
