# Creamy Italian Chicken & Broccoli Pasta

A creamy chicken-and-vegetable pasta prepared in two stages using the Power Pressure Cooker XL.

**Equipment:** Power Pressure Cooker XL  
**Servings:** 8  
**Total:** 60–75 minutes  
**Cooking method:** One pot / two-stage cook

## Ingredients

- 3 lb bone-in chicken leg quarters
- 1 lb whole-grain pasta (16 oz)
- 1 bag frozen broccoli florets (about 12 oz)
- 1 bag frozen mixed vegetables (about 12 oz)
- 1 can diced tomatoes (14–15 oz)
- 1 medium yellow onion, chopped
- 2½ cups chicken broth for the chicken
- Extra broth or water for pasta (about 4 cups total pasta cooking liquid)
- ¾ cup milk
- ¾ cup shredded sharp cheddar
- 2 teaspoons Italian seasoning
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- ½ teaspoon smoked paprika
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Power Pressure Cooker XL directions

1. **Load cooker.** Add chicken, onion, 2½ cups broth, Italian seasoning, garlic powder, and smoked paprika.
2. **Pressure-cook.** Secure lid. Cook on Meat/Chicken for about 18–20 minutes; allow 10–15 minutes natural release, then vent safely.
3. **Shred.** Check chicken reaches **165°F**. Remove bones and skin, shred meat, and reserve broth.
4. **Start pasta.** Add about 4 cups of reserved broth and/or water to inner pot. Add pasta and tomatoes.
5. **Simmer uncovered.** Use Sauté, stirring regularly for about 10–15 minutes. Add liquid as necessary.
6. **Add vegetables.** Stir in broccoli and mixed vegetables when pasta is nearly tender. Simmer until pasta is cooked and vegetables hot.
7. **Make creamy.** Reduce heat. Stir in milk and cheddar until combined.
8. **Finish.** Fold in chicken, heat thoroughly, and season to taste. Thin with broth or milk if needed.

## Leftovers and cooker notes

Makes approximately eight servings for two days of lunches and dinners. Reheat portions with 1–2 tablespoons milk or water to loosen sauce. **Do not pressure-cook pasta in this XL**; use uncovered Sauté. Keep pot within fill limits and refrigerate leftovers promptly.
