# Smoky Salsa Chicken, Rice & Bean Bowls

A hearty, flavorful batch meal with shredded chicken, rice, beans, vegetables, salsa, and green chiles. Great for meal prep and leftovers.

**Equipment:** Power Pressure Cooker XL  
**Servings:** 8–10  
**Time:** 75–90 minutes  
**Historical estimated batch cost:** $13–16

## Ingredients

- 4 lb chicken leg quarters, bone-in
- 1½ cups dry white rice, rinsed
- 2 cans (15 oz each) pinto beans, drained
- 2 cans (15 oz each) black beans, drained
- 1 can diced tomatoes (14–15 oz)
- 1 jar salsa (about 16 oz)
- 1 can diced green chiles (4–7 oz), to taste
- 1 bag frozen mixed vegetables (about 12 oz)
- ⅓ head green cabbage, finely shredded (optional)
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 1 cup shredded cheddar, for serving
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- ½ teaspoon dried oregano
- 2 cups chicken broth, plus cooking broth from chicken
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Power Pressure Cooker XL directions

1. **Cook chicken.** Add chicken, onion, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, oregano, and 2 cups broth to cooker. Secure lid and pressure-cook on Meat/Chicken for 18–22 minutes. If necessary, cook chicken in two rounds to avoid overfilling.
2. **Natural release.** Allow 10–15 minutes natural pressure release, then release remaining pressure safely. Remove chicken, check **165°F**, discard skin and bones, shred meat, and reserve broth.
3. **Cook rice.** Measure about 2 cups of reserved broth into inner pot (adjust to rice directions). Add rinsed rice and cook on Rice setting approximately 6 minutes, followed by 10-minute natural release. Fluff rice.
4. **Heat filling.** With lid off, use Sauté to heat pinto and black beans, diced tomatoes, salsa, green chiles, mixed vegetables, and optional cabbage until hot and tender.
5. **Combine.** Stir shredded chicken into bean mixture. Fold in cooked rice, in cooker if it fits or in a large bowl.
6. **Serve.** Top with shredded cheddar and serve hot. Add extra salsa or green chile for heat.

## Serving, leftovers and safety

Serve as rice bowls with salsa and cheddar; add plain yogurt or sour cream if desired. Also good with toast, tortillas or a simple side salad.

Great for two days of lunch and dinner. Reheat with a splash of broth or water to keep rice moist; reheat leftovers to **165°F**. Keep rice and beans within the cooker fill limits. Cook chicken first, then rice, then combine.
