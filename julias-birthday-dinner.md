# Julia's August 28, 2026 Birthday Dinner

## Greek Salad — serves 2

### Ingredients

* 1 small romaine heart, chopped
* 1 cup cherry or grape tomatoes, halved
* ½ cucumber, sliced or chopped
* ¼ small red onion, very thinly sliced
* ¼ cup Kalamata or other Greek-style olives
* ¼ cup crumbled feta cheese
* 4–6 pepperoncini, optional
* A few thin strips roasted red pepper, optional

### Simple Greek dressing

* 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
* 1½ tbsp red wine vinegar
* ½ tsp dried oregano
* ½ small garlic clove, finely minced or grated
* ¼ tsp Dijon mustard
* Small pinch salt
* Freshly ground black pepper

Whisk the dressing together until combined. Toss the lettuce, tomatoes, cucumber, onion, olives, and pepperoncini with just enough dressing to coat. Add the feta at the end so it stays distinct instead of dissolving into the dressing.

For your dinner, I’d keep the salad **lightly dressed** because the lasagna is already rich.

# Classic Italian-American Lasagna

### Adjusted to 1 lb ground beef

This is an adapted version of the cookbook recipe you photographed, with the beef reduced from 1½ lb to **1 lb**.

## Ingredients

### Meat sauce

* **1 lb lean ground beef**
* 1 onion, chopped
* 2 celery stalks, chopped
* ½ red bell pepper, chopped
* 2 garlic cloves, minced
* 1 tbsp chopped fresh basil
* 1 tsp dried oregano
* 1 tsp dried thyme
* 1½ tsp salt
* 1 can diced tomatoes with juice, about 28 oz
* 2 cans tomato paste, 6 oz each

### Pasta

* 12 dry lasagna noodles

### Ricotta filling

* 2 eggs, beaten
* 2 cups part-skim ricotta
* ⅓ cup grated Parmesan
* 2 tbsp dried parsley
* 1 tsp salt

### Assembly

* 1 lb shredded mozzarella
* 4 tbsp grated Parmesan for the top
* Small amount of oil for the baking dish

---

## 1. Make the meat sauce

Heat a large skillet over medium heat.

Add the **1 lb ground beef**, chopped onion, celery, red bell pepper, and garlic.

Cook until the beef is browned and the vegetables have softened. Break the meat into small pieces as it cooks.

Drain excess fat if necessary.

Stir in:

* fresh basil
* oregano
* thyme
* 1½ tsp salt
* diced tomatoes with their juice
* tomato paste

Mix thoroughly.

Bring to a gentle simmer, then reduce the heat and cook for about **30–45 minutes**, stirring occasionally.

Because we're using only 1 lb of beef, you'll end up with a slightly more tomato-forward sauce, which I think will actually work very well here.

---

## 2. Cook the noodles

While the sauce simmers, preheat the oven to **375°F**.

Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil.

Add the 12 lasagna noodles and cook until tender but still firm enough to hold their shape, roughly **9 minutes** depending on the brand.

Drain them.

Lay the noodles flat on clean kitchen towels or parchment so they don't stick together.

---

## 3. Make the ricotta mixture

In a medium bowl, combine:

* 2 beaten eggs
* 2 cups ricotta
* ⅓ cup Parmesan
* 2 tbsp dried parsley
* 1 tsp salt

Mix until evenly combined.

---

# 4. Assemble the lasagna

Lightly oil the bottom and sides of a **9 × 13-inch baking dish**.

You're going to build layers.

### First layer

Place about **⅓ of the lasagna noodles** in the bottom.

Then add approximately:

* ½ of the ricotta mixture
* ½ of the mozzarella
* ⅓ of the meat sauce

### Second layer

Repeat with:

* another ⅓ of the noodles
* remaining ricotta mixture
* remaining mozzarella
* another ⅓ of the sauce

### Top layer

Finish with:

* remaining noodles
* remaining meat sauce
* 4 tbsp grated Parmesan

Try to spread the final sauce all the way to the edges so the exposed noodles don't dry out.

---

# 5. Bake

Bake uncovered at **375°F for about 30 minutes**.

You're looking for:

* bubbling sauce around the edges
* melted cheese
* a lightly browned top

---

# 6. The important part: let it rest

Take the lasagna out and let it sit for **at least 10 minutes** before cutting.

For the cleaner restaurant-style pieces we were discussing, I'd give it **12–15 minutes**.

That resting time allows the cheese and sauce to settle so the layers stay together when you lift the slice.

## For tonight

Given the rest of your meal, I’d serve a **moderate rectangular slice**, not an enormous one, with:

* a little Parmesan on top
* 2–3 fresh basil leaves
* Greek salad on a separate small plate or bowl
* garlic Parmesan bread on the side/table

One small note: the salad we originally planned was an **Italian garden salad** rather than Greek. The Greek salad above will still pair very well with the lasagna, but if “Greek” was a slip and you want the original Italian one, I can give you that exact recipe too.
